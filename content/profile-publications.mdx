# Selected writing and recognition

## Open-source LLM landscape

Pankit co-authored "Exploring the Landscape of the Open Source Large Language Model," published at the International Conference on Web Services, Telecommunications, and Computing in 2023. The paper surveys open-source large-language-model development and its practical implications.

## Hackathon recognition

He was a runner-up at the 2023 AU-IQM Hackathon for an AI-powered traffic-management project that applied YOLO-based detection to support adaptive traffic-signal decisions.
