# Research overview

## Central research question

How can an agent turn remembered or retrieved evidence into a reliable action when sources can be incomplete, stale, conflicting, or wrong? The portfolio approaches this as an end-to-end systems question spanning memory, retrieval, adaptation, reasoning, tools, and evaluation.

## Research method

The projects make evidence boundaries visible, record provenance and time, test interventions or counterfactuals where possible, and distinguish deterministic software checks from broader research claims. They favor calibrated clarification or abstention when the indexed evidence is insufficient.

## Shared system view

The shared framing is memory as a decision system. Across the portfolio, reliable behavior depends on more than storing facts: an agent must recognize relevant evidence, recall it at the right moment, identify its source, decide whether it is still valid, and use it to choose an appropriate action.
